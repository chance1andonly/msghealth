# What MsgHealth actually is (use for explainer reels; do not claim more)
Client-retention and win-back texting app for Atlanta hairstylists and barbers. "Know your customers. Keep your customers."
Does: client health scoring (who's active vs drifting), churn-risk detection, automatic win-back texts/emails to clients who've gone quiet, booking and appointment management, review requests, loyalty/retention, payments and invoicing, analytics, a shared inbox, automations, and a human rep to contact.
Explainer arc: score every client -> text the quiet ones -> they reply and book -> you just cut hair. CTA: Comment KEEP and we'll show you who's gone quiet.
Never promise revenue numbers or results we can't back up.

## Owner-provided facts (2026-11-27; claim only these)
Support is nationwide (do not lead with Atlanta/local). Pricing: Starter $50/mo, Pro $109/mo (most popular), Enterprise $279/mo, plus pay-per-use wallet (texts $0.04 each). Owner states win-back is included on every plan; NOTE the plan list shows "Retention Autopilot" under Pro, Starter lists health scoring + churn-risk flags + broadcast campaigns + 300 SMS/email reminders/mo, so confirm before claiming automatic win-back on Starter.
Health score (0-100, every plan): each client is compared with THEIR OWN usual visit rhythm (recency-weighted average of up to last 16 gaps), not a fixed rule like "90 days". Six factors: overdue vs own rhythm (up to -30), recent visit rhythm (-50 to +20), booking trend (-10 to +10), no-shows (-6 each, max -20), client cancellations (-5 each, max -20), reply speed vs their own habit (up to -15). Bands: Healthy 70-100, At risk 40-69, High priority 0-39. Dashboard "Why is this customer at risk?" shows every factor with real numbers. Updates instantly on booking changes and nightly.
Separate churn-risk score 0-100 (Low/Medium/High/Critical) weighs overdue, 30+ days no contact, tone of texts, score drops, missed appointments; safeguard: texting a client yourself never counts as them being engaged. AI-enhanced score on Pro+ adds text sentiment, star ratings, overdue invoices, spending trend, email opens, campaign replies.
No customer results/revenue numbers are available; never promise outcomes.
