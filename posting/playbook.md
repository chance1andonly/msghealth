# Niche playbook (rewritten by the batch task from posting/research.json and posting/stats.json)

Updated 2026-11-18 from the first working research run (10 accounts, 15 outliers) and our own views.

## What the niche's outliers have in common (views ÷ that account's median)
- Emotional payoff held to the end: "free haircut for a 102 year old veteran", "10 year old said something I didn't expect, wait for the end". 18x to 19x. The caption promises a reaction, the video delivers it last.
- A concrete, odd specific as the hook: "got kicked out for cutting hair", "snuck into the CEO's office". Not a category, a scene.
- A comment-word CTA that gives something ("comment LINK", message "CONTENT" for free training). It doubles as the engagement signal.
- A poll or question in the caption ("vote below", "rate the transformation").
- Most of the biggest outliers speak to clients, not to barbers. Our viewer is the barber, so copy the structure (specific hook, payoff at the end, one-word CTA), not the topic.

## What our own reels say (views)
- Top: Pentagon pizza (1,708), coffin ship (1,289), London time lady (794). All open with a surprising true story and pivot to retention.
- The direct "you're losing regulars" reels do about half as well.

## Rules for new scripts
1. Hook = one odd, true, specific scene with a number, under 14 words.
2. Hold the payoff (the twist or the lesson) until the line before the CTA.
3. Keep the one-word CTA, and offer something for it.
4. Story first, barber lesson second.
5. Follow posting/VOICE.md so it sounds like a person.
